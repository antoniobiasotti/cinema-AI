### We are in the midst of a new Renaissance right now, and the reason for it is data!
In the past 2 years, we have created more data than in the entire previous history of the human race. In face of this tremendous amount of data, we often find ourselves having a hard time making a simple decision. This uncertainty is often called <i>The Paradox of Choice</i>, in which we can't make up our minds due to the fact that there are so manny choices!<br>
To solve this problem, recommendation engines were created. 
Basically, there are two main types of Recommender Systems: 
- Collaborative: Predicts what you like based on what other similar user have liked in the past;
- Content-based: Predicts what you like based on what you liked in the past.

Netflix, for instance, combines this two approaches.

In this project I created a simple script that reads a dataset of movie ratings and then recommends new movies for a user.</p>
